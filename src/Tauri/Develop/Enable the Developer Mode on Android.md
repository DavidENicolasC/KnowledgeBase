Each Android distribution has its own way to enable it, but the general steps are this:

Connect your Android device to the computer, open the **Settings** app on the Android device, select **About**, scroll to Build Number, and tap it 7 times. This will enable Developer Mode for your Android device and the **Developer Options** settings.

To enable application debugging on your device, you must enter the **Developer Options** settings, toggle on the developer options switch and enable **USB Debugging**.
